# QC Control 1.0.0

Meet your tiny desk companion: a smiling Japanese Spitz with headphones, and one-click control over your listening environment.

- Switch Quiet, Aware, Immersion, and configured custom modes from the menu bar.
- Adjust noise cancellation with a 0–10 slider.
- Choose a battery percentage, ring, dots, or the Spitz mascot, with optional native animation.
- Reconnect automatically and optionally launch at login.
- Communicate directly with your headphones over Bluetooth, with no account or telemetry.

## Requirements

macOS 13 or later. Bose QuietComfort Ultra Headphones (first generation, firmware 1.6.7) are the currently hardware-verified model. Other models are not yet verified.

## Install

Download `QC-Control-1.0.0-universal.zip`, unzip it, move **QC Control.app** to Applications, then open it and allow Bluetooth access. Pair the headphones in macOS Bluetooth Settings first.

## Notes

Aware mode lets outside sound in; this model has no separate ANC-off switch. Adjusting the slider creates or updates a dedicated QC Control mode without overwriting other named modes. The app icon is static; the menu bar mascot can animate.

QC Control is an independent, unofficial utility and is not affiliated with Bose.
